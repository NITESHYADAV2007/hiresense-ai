balance

Built for honest hiring

HireSense AI is a demonstration project. It shows how resume parsing and text-similarity scoring can support a recruitment workflow — while being explicit about what such scores cannot tell you. Scores are never used to automatically reject people, and no protected or personal traits are inferred from resumes.

shield_lock

Candidate privacy

Applications collect only what a recruiter needs to evaluate a candidate: name, contact details, and a resume. Resumes are stored outside the public web root and are only downloadable by the authenticated recruiter who owns the job. Candidates can request deletion of their record at any time through the hiring organisation.
