Writing a matchable job

  • Use the real job title candidates would search for — it is compared against resume text.
  • Describe responsibilities in plain language; the similarity score works on this description.
  • List concrete skills (e.g. “React”, “PostgreSQL”) — each one is checked against every resume.
  • Avoid sensitive or personal traits. Only include job-related requirements.
  • The match score is decision support — it never auto-rejects anyone.

Example skills line:
React, Node.js, SQL, Docker, REST
